This release changes what error reports contain, so I can fix a problem from the report alone. Error reports still go only to my own error tracker on our server in Germany, and the privacy policy now says exactly what they hold. Nothing changes in how you use the app.

## Fixed

- **Error reports contain the request that failed.** When the server hits a problem, the report now includes the page address, what was sent with it, your browser or app version, the steps the server took just before, and your account's number and username. If you were saving a log, that can include its times, intensity and doses, which are health data, processed under the consent you already gave and only to fix the problem. What you write (medications, triggers and notes), passwords, cookies and IP addresses are still left out. By @crmne
- **The privacy policy says what error reports hold and how to have them deleted.** In English, German, Italian and Spanish. Error reports aren't deleted with your account; email me with your username and I'll delete them. By @crmne
