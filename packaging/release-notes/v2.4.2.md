This release finishes the privacy work of the last two: a share link no longer shows anything until its owner has consented to my keeping their health data. It also makes room for the Android app's new floating toolbar. Nothing changes in how you log an attack.

## Fixed

- **Share links wait for your consent.** If you haven't yet consented to the processing of your health data, a link you shared now says it's paused and asks you to open the app, instead of showing your logs, charts and PDF. The link works again once you consent, until it expires. By @crmne
- **Room for the Android app's floating toolbar.** Logs, Charts, Account and Feedback leave space under their last row for the tabs and the New log button that the next version of the Android app floats over them. The website and the iOS app look the same as before. By @crmne in [#227](https://github.com/crmne/cluster-headache-tracker/pull/227)
- **An updated CSS sanitizer.** The crass library is now at 1.0.8, which fixes a published advisory. By @crmne in [#228](https://github.com/crmne/cluster-headache-tracker/pull/228)
